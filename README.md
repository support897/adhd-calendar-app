# ADHD Calendar App

Ilse's project: a calendar app in the Fantastical aesthetic, built for people with ADHD and overwhelm. A morning "how are you feeling today" check in three levels and colours adapts the day (bare minimum on a bad day); a talk-to-it side menu turns journals, voice recordings, handwriting and notes into an organised to-do list with colours and times; a progress menu shows what is done and what is left. Apple iPhone intuitive, interactive, drag and drop.

Her full prompt: PROMPT.md. Phase 1 is research (Fantastical aesthetic, ADHD and overwhelm psychology, best existing GitHub base to copy and adapt).
