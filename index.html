Welcome to your calm calendar

    
Each morning it asks how you are feeling, in three levels. The day then shapes itself: small on a low day, full on a full day. Nothing is ever marked late or missed. Things that do not fit are parked, safe, and easy to find.

    
A few sample tasks are in today so you can see how it works. Remove them any time in Settings.

    
Notifications are gentle and you choose them. Want the morning check-in reminder?
